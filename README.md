# IDRA Day 14 - Food Delivery Data Visualization Portfolio

## Assignment

Food Delivery Business Performance Data Visualization using Python, Matplotlib and Seaborn.

## Organization

India Data Research Academy (IDRA)

## Description

This project focuses on exploring and analyzing a Food Delivery Business Performance Dataset using different data visualization techniques.

The objective is to identify meaningful patterns and communicate business insights related to orders, revenue, cities, cuisines, marketing spend, delivery time, customer ratings, weather conditions and order channels.

## Dataset

The dataset contains 360 rows and 16 columns.

The dataset includes information related to:

- Date
- City
- Cuisine
- Orders
- Average Order Value
- Revenue
- Marketing Spend
- Discounts
- Average Delivery Minutes
- Customer Rating
- Repeat Customer Percentage
- Weather
- Order Channel
- And other business performance variables

## Data Inspection

The dataset was checked for:

- Dataset shape
- Column names
- Data types
- Statistical summary
- Missing values
- Duplicate records

There were no missing values and no duplicate records in the dataset.

## Key Findings

- Bengaluru generated the highest total revenue among the cities.
- Delhi was another strong revenue-generating city.
- Healthy cuisine recorded the highest number of orders.
- Healthy cuisine also generated the highest total revenue.
- The App was the leading order channel and generated the highest revenue.
- Orders and Revenue showed a strong positive relationship.
- Marketing Spend showed a relatively weak positive relationship with Revenue.
- Rainy weather resulted in the highest average delivery time.
- Rainy weather also showed lower average customer ratings.
- Weather appears to have a clearer effect on delivery operations than on total revenue.
- Order volume is an important factor associated with revenue.
